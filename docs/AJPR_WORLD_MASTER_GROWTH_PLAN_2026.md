## 3. Algorithmic Optimization Checklist (Zero Penalty Execution)

1. **No External Links in Body Copy**:
   - LinkedIn severely penalizes posts containing outbound links (up to 60% reach suppression).
   - *Rule*: All links to `ajprworld.com` are placed in the **First Comment** within 60 seconds of publishing.
2. **Visual Format Preference (PDF Carousels)**:
   - Document/Carousel posts generate **3x to 5x more dwell time** than single images or text-only updates.
   - Every weekly post is paired with a 5-to-7 slide high-contrast carousel deck (4:5 ratio).
3. **The "Golden Hour" Founder-to-Company Loop**:
   - Step 1: AJPR World page publishes the post.
   - Step 2: Aman Joshi's personal profile adds the first comment with founder perspective and tags key collaborators.
   - Step 3: Aman Joshi reposts with "Thoughts on this breakdown..." to tap into personal network reach.
4. **Hashtag Strategy**:
   - Exactly 3 to 5 targeted hashtags: `#B2BMarketing #GrowthEngineering #ProgrammaticSEO #SoftwareEngineering #AJPRWorld`.
